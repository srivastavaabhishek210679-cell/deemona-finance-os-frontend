Privacy Policy

Deemona AI Finance OS is operated by Deemona Global Solutions. Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how Deemona Global Solutions handles personal data in Deemona AI Finance OS, under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the SPDI Rules, 2011 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), as they apply to us. For business data that Customers upload, we act largely as a processor on the Customer's instructions; see Data Processing.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, work email, phone, company name, role, password hash, 2FA settings.
  • Customer Data you add or connect: invoices, ledgers, bank transactions, payroll and employee records, vendor and customer details, GST and tax information, uploaded files, and data pulled from connected systems.
  • Connector credentials: API keys and tokens you provide so we can connect to other systems.
  • AI interactions: questions and prompts to the Digital CFO and the answers produced.
  • Usage and security data: IP address, device and browser, logs, audit-trail entries, cookies (see Cookie Policy).
  • Billing data: billing contact, GSTIN, payment status from payment partners. We do not store full card details.

2. How we use it

  • To provide, secure and support the Service, including reports, alerts and scheduled emails.
  • To produce AI outputs you request or have scheduled.
  • To send service messages (OTPs, alerts, reports).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, keep audit records, meet legal duties, and improve reliability.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your Customer Data to train our own models.

3. Who we share it with

Only with service providers needed to run the Service, under confidentiality duties:

  • Cloud hosting and database providers
  • Email delivery providers
  • AI model providers that process prompts and the data needed to answer them
  • Payment partners (for billing)
  • Third-party systems you choose to connect

We also disclose data where required by law, court order or to protect rights and safety.

4. Security

We use tenant separation, role-based sign-in controls, encrypted connections, rate limiting, audit logging and optional two-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure; you should also use strong passwords and limit user access.

5. Storage and transfers

Data may be stored and processed on cloud infrastructure located in or outside India, including Singapore. Where law requires, transfers follow the safeguards it prescribes.

6. Retention

See the Data Retention Policy.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, erase or export your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate a representative. Employees or customers of a Customer should first contact that Customer, who controls their data; we will help the Customer respond. Write to the Grievance Officer. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

8. Breach notice

If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify the affected Customer and, where required, the authorities.

9. Children

The Service is for business use by adults. See Child Safety.

10. Changes and contact

We will post updates here and notify material changes.
