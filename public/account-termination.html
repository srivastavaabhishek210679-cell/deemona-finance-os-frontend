Account Termination and Deletion

Deemona AI Finance OS is operated by Deemona Global Solutions. Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Customer cancellation

The company administrator can cancel the subscription at any time by emailing abhishek240575@gmail.com from the registered admin email, or through the app where available. Billing stops at the end of the paid period, subject to the Refund Policy.

2. Export and deletion

After cancellation the Customer can export its data for 30 days. After that we delete or anonymise Customer Data as per the Data Retention Policy. A user can also ask to delete their personal account data by writing to us; we verify the request and complete it within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law.

3. Suspension or termination by us

We may suspend or end access, with notice where practical, if:

  • the Terms or Acceptable Use Policy are breached;
  • invoices stay unpaid after notice (see Payment Terms);
  • there is fraud, a security threat, or risk to other tenants;
  • the law or an authority requires it;
  • the account is inactive for a long period (we will warn first).

For serious or urgent threats we may suspend first and explain after.

4. Effect

On termination access ends, scheduled reports and syncs stop, and connector credentials are removed. Clauses that by nature continue (liability, confidentiality, payment due) survive.

5. Appeals

If you think action was a mistake, write to the Grievance Officer. We reply within 15 days.
