Data Processing and Connector Terms

Deemona AI Finance OS is operated by Deemona Global Solutions. Last updated: 27 October 2026

This page applies when a Customer puts personal data of others (employees, customers, vendors) into Deemona AI Finance OS. It forms part of the Terms of Service.

1. Roles

The Customer decides what data is processed and why, and is the data fiduciary or controller. Deemona Global Solutions is the processor and acts only on the Customer's documented instructions (using the Service, configuring connectors, and written requests).

2. Our commitments

  • Process Customer Data only to provide and support the Service and as the law requires.
  • Keep it confidential; allow access only to staff who need it.
  • Keep tenant data logically separated and apply the security measures in the Privacy Policy.
  • Use sub-processors (hosting, database, email, AI providers) under written terms, and tell Customers of material changes on request.
  • Help the Customer respond to data subject requests and notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach affecting its data.
  • On termination, make Customer Data available for export for 30 days, then delete it as per the Data Retention Policy.

3. Customer commitments

  • Have a lawful basis and any needed consent or notice before loading personal data.
  • Keep user access and roles current, and remove leavers promptly.
  • Keep connector credentials valid and give us only the access needed.

4. Connectors

Credentials supplied for connectors are used only to sync the data you configure. Revoke them at the source system at any time to stop syncing. Sync scope and frequency are controlled in the app.

5. AI processing

When AI features run, relevant Customer Data and your prompt are sent to our AI provider to produce the answer. Calculations are done by the platform where possible; the AI explains results.
